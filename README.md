        <h1>Description of Apps</h1>
        <div id="instructions-text">
	  CHEMCENTRATION is an app to reinforce knowledge of the first 20 elements:
          1. The board has 40 tiles hiding element names and symbols.<br>
          2. Click a tile to reveal it. Then click a second tile to find its
             correponding name/symbol.<br>
          3. If they match, they turn green and reveal part of a hidden picture.<br>
          4. If they do not match, they turn red and flip back over.<br>
          5. Reveal the entire picture as fast as you can to make the Leader Board!<br>
          6. To pause, click Exit Game, and click Cancel when ready to continue.<br><br>
        </div>
        <div id="instructions-text">
	  STOICHIOMETRY TRAINER is an app to reinforce knowledge of this critical
	    chemistry topic:<br>
          1. You first face <u>Step Ordering</u> where you drag and drop the
	     steps of solving a stoichiometry problems.<br>
          2. Next you have <u>Problem Solver</u>, the chance to solve actual problems.<br>
	  3. Select one and you must first balance the relavent equation.<br>
	  4. You next need to choose whether you want to solve the problem without
	     or with assistance.<br>
	  5. Without assistance means you only have the problem and answer box.<br>
	     However, if you are off by more than 10% you have the opportunity
	     to switch to Assisted Mode.<br>
	  6. Finally, you can see your top 10 scores in either Step Ordering or
	     Balancing equations.<br>
