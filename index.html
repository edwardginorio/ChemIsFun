ChemIsFun Applications

Description of Apps

CHEMCENTRATION is an app to reinforce knowledge of the first 20 elements: 1. The board has 40 tiles hiding element names and symbols.
2. Click a tile to reveal it. Then click a second tile to find its correponding name/symbol.
3. If they match, they turn green and reveal part of a hidden picture.
4. If they do not match, they turn red and flip back over.
5. Reveal the entire picture as fast as you can to make the Leader Board!
6. To pause, click Exit Game, and click Cancel when ready to continue.

STOICHIOMETRY TRAINER is an app to reinforce knowledge of this critical chemistry topic:
1. You first face Step Ordering where you drag and drop the steps of solving a stoichiometry problems.
2. Next you have Problem Solver, the chance to solve actual problems.
3. Select one and you must first balance the relavent equation.
4. You next need to choose whether you want to solve the problem without or with assistance.
5. Without assistance means you only have the problem and answer box.
However, if you are off by more than 10% you have the opportunity to switch to Assisted Mode.
6. Finally, you can see your top 10 scores in either Step Ordering or Balancing equations.
