How to Play

1. The board has 40 tiles hiding element names and symbols.

2. Click a tile to reveal it. Then click a second tile to find its match.

3. If they match, they turn green and reveal part of a hidden picture.

4. If they don't match, they turn red and flip back over.

5. Reveal the entire picture as fast as you can to make the Leader Board!
