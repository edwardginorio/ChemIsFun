How to Play

Stoichiometry is simply accounting for chemical reactions. Almost every stoichiometry problem boils down to a single core rule: you cannot compare substances using grams or liters directly—you must convert through moles.

Think of the mole as the central bridge. Whatever units you start with, convert to moles first, use the reaction coefficients to bridge across to your target substance, and then convert out to your final unit.

Prerequisite for mole ratios: You cannot calculate stoichiometry without balanced equation coefficients. Make sure the number of atoms of each element is equal on both sides of the arrow.

If you need practice balancing equations try:

Open PhET Balancing Simulator

2. Problem Solver: Practice your skills with some problems!

First, you select a problem. Then you have to balance the associated equation. At thst point you have a decision to , make, whether to attempt to solve the problem with assistance or without.

Unassisted only presents the problem and a field for the answer.

Assisted will take you through the solution process step by step.

  • From Mass (g): Divide by molar mass (g/mol).
  • From Volume of Gas (L at STP): Divide by 22.4 L/mol.
  • From Particles/Molecules: Divide by Avogadro's number (6.022 × 1023).

Now you must apply the mole ratio that is comprised of the moles of what you are looking for over the moles of what you started with.

Convert your target substance (Substance B) into whatever unit the prompt asks for:

  • To Mass (g): Multiply by molar mass (g/mol).
  • To Volume (L at STP): Multiply by 22.4 L/mol.
  • To Number of Particles: Multiply by 6.022 × 1023.

NOTE: If either side is in moles, NO conversion is necessary!

Finally you calculate the formula presented.

For example:
10 grams H₂ / 2 g/mol ) × (2 mol H₂O / 2 mol H₂) × 18 g/m)

First multply the numerators: 10 × 2 × 18 = 360
Then multply the denominators: 2 × 2 × 1 = 4
Now divide the numerators by the denominators:
360 / 4 = 90(g) which is the answer (only enter numbers)

If you try Assisted Mode and enter a different answer 3 times, you can press Show Correct Answer to see the correct answer.

In Unassisted Mode, if your answer is more than 10% off, you will be given the opportunity to change to Assisted Mode.
